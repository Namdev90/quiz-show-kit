## Spoken option letters

`assets/audio/optionA.wav` through `optionD.wav`, and their embedded copies in `src/shared-assets.json`, were supplied for the BioVista event after local generation using the installed Microsoft Zira voice through the Windows Speech API. Silence was trimmed and levels normalized. Playback uses the embedded recordings; it does not contact a speech service or request microphone access.

These recordings are not relicensed under the project's MIT software license, and this notice makes no independent license grant for the voice or recordings. Retain their provenance. For a different distribution, use recordings for which you have the required rights; the corresponding asset keys can be replaced in the configuration.
